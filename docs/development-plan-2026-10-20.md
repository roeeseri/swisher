# Near-term development plan

1. Export one clip per proposed shot with preparation, follow-through and landing; retain source timestamps. Provide approve/delete/adjust/add-missed-shot controls.
2. Label approximately 50 attempts across players and views. Keep players disjoint between development and evaluation. Label shot bounds, release interval and outcome only when visible.
3. Evaluate shot recall, false proposals and release timing separately. Do not equate proposal counts with accuracy.
4. Validate three descriptive measures: knee bend, body-to-arm timing, and follow-through. Compare within player and view. Abstain when joints are occluded; 2D angles are camera-dependent.
5. Produce player review reports with comparable clips. Obtain coach review before causal advice or claims of improvement.

Status: this plan is not yet implemented. Experimental detection, camera-region tracking, event overlays, batch reporting and provisional release evaluation are implemented.
